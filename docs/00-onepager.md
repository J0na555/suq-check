# SuqCheck — one-pager

**Weekly price-compliance infrastructure for Addis staples.**
Ambassadors resolve 40 SKUs × 120 shops every week; brands subscribe to the feed.

---

## Twin headlines

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **5.9 ETB** | Fully loaded cost per verified observation (capped reward pool), vs **3,160–6,320 ETB** for a WFP face-to-face price questionnaire and **1,550–3,900 ETB** for a Field Agent–class store mission (international upper bound). Derivation: [05-money.md](05-money.md). |

## The machine

- **8 ambassadors** × **15 shops** × weekly routes in **Bole, Yeka, Arada** (40 shops each).  
- Visit **12–18 min**: GPS check-in, photo of the legally posted price list (**Proclamation 813/2013 Art. 15(1)**; Addis **Directive 159/2024**), shelf zones, out-of-stock flags.  
- **~28 prices + ~12 OOS** per shop → **4,800 cells resolved**/week (not 4,800 prices — yield is ~70% priced).  
- **90 ETB**/approved visit; crowd adds receipts and extra shops for points → telebirr/airtime (**1,000 pts = 100 ETB**).  
- **Coverage does not depend on consumer adoption.** Crowd is upside. (Esoko lesson: [02-comparables-evidence.md](02-comparables-evidence.md).)
