## Revenue layers

| Layer | Who pays | What they buy | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Brand subscriptions** | Manufacturers / brand owners | Pulse / Signal / Signal Pro weekly feeds | Primary cash from month 4 |
| **Gap-task campaigns** | Brand trade/marketing budgets | Targeted SKU×shop captures (Fetch pattern) | From month 6–9 |
| **Launch audits** | Brand / distributor | One-off 120-shop new-SKU checks | Opportunistic |
| **Institutional / development** | WFP, WB RTP-style, CSA, Trade Bureau | Aggregated staples panel, delayed procurement | Pipeline month 1; cash ~month 9–12 |
| **Retailer SaaS** | Shops | **Not a paid wedge** — free "price-posted / fair shelf" badge programme | Soft power for shop access |

Brand-funded rewards (campaign pools + 30% fee) keep variable crowd cost off the core opex line when possible.

## Sequencing

**Brands first for cash. Institutional first for pipeline.**

| Track | Start | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design-partner pilot (Samanu or Phibela) | Month 1 | Free 3-month / 50% structure per price card; logo rights |
| Paying Pulse/Signal | Month 4–6 | Break-even maths in [05](05-money.md) |
| Institutional conversations (WFP, WB RTP, CSA, Trade Bureau) | **Month 1** | Procurement takes **6–12 months**; waiting until profitable means year-two cash |
| Retailer badge programme | Month 2–3 | Shop-access mitigation, not MRR |
| Consumer growth | Ongoing | Upside freshness; never gate coverage on DAU |

Retailer SaaS as a **paid** product is explicitly deferred — charging shops fights the access problem Directive 159/2024 already creates.

## Trust firewall

### Brand-facing neutrality

- Same panel, same engine, same weights for every subscriber.  
- No exclusive SKU suppression: a Signal Pro buyer does not get competitors scrubbed.  
- Methodology card shipped with every pilot (source mix, gate band ±35%, freshness decay).

### Shop-facing confidentiality

- Individual shop identities in brand exports default to **district-level** on Pulse; shop-level only on Signal Pro with contractual limits on enforcement use.  
- Written commitment: SuqCheck **does not** feed Trade Bureau enforcement from ambassador photos.  
- Ambassadors carry ID + explainer letter; photography framed as market research, not inspection.

### No-exclusivity policy

- No category exclusivity sold in year one.  
- Geographic exclusivity (e.g. "only Unilever sees Bole") is refused — it destroys the shared-fixed-cost margin story.  
- Campaign data from a brand-funded gap task may be time-embargoed (**[ASSUMPTION] 2–4 weeks**) before entering the syndicated panel.

## KPI definitions

| KPI | Definition | Target (steady, months 4–6) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shop coverage | Share of 120 shops with ≥1 approved visit in the ISO week | **100%** (refusals replaced from reserve list) |
| Cell resolution | Share of 4,800 cells with price **or** verified OOS | **100%** |
| Price yield | Priced observations / 4,800 | **~70%** (~3,360) |
| Approval rate | Approved ambassador visits / submitted | **≥95%** (bonus threshold) |
| Pending rate | Evidence in pending / ingested | Monitor; spike = training or shock |
| Median confidence | Market estimates on basket SKUs | **≥85** on rich SKUs; thin/stale SKUs kept visible on purpose |
| Crowd share | Crowd obs / total obs | **~20%** upside ([ASSUMPTION] 4k/18.5k) |
| Reward burn | ETB paid / crowd obs | **~0.7 expected**; ceiling per [05](05-money.md) |
| Logo NR | Paying brand subscriptions | **2** by month 6 (~2.1 after WH) |
| Cash runway | Months to trough recovery | Funded per [05](05-money.md) §9 |

Vanity DAU is not a primary KPI for the panel business.
