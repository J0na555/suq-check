# 06 — Implementation (90 days)

Week-by-week launch, scripted sales, partnerships, do-not-do list, risks. Costs in [05-money.md](05-money.md); ops in [04-operating-model.md](04-operating-model.md).

## Week-by-week sequence

### Weeks 1–2 — Panel skeleton

- Freeze 40-SKU basket via 10-shop facing pre-audit.  
- Register 120 shops (40 Bole / 40 Yeka / 40 Arada) + 15-shop reserve per district.  
- Hire 8 ambassadors + PT coordinator; train visit protocol and shop letter.  
- Legal pack: 813/2013 Art. 15 explainer, non-enforcement letter, ID badges.  
- Ship ingest path for **price-list photo** (flagship) alongside receipt/shelf.

### Weeks 3–4 — First full refresh

- Run all 120 visits; measure refusal rate (budget **10%**).  
- Clear pending queue daily; publish internal methodology card.  
- Open design-partner pilot with **Samanu** (fallback Phibela): free/50% per price card.  
- Start institutional intro emails (pipeline only).

### Weeks 5–8 — Reliability

- Spot-audit 10%/week; coach or replace under-performers.  
- Turn on crowd receipts with **airtime** payouts first (manual telebirr OK).  
- Cap reward pool; steer 60% to gap bounties on thin SKUs.  
- Dashboard: pulse, ingestion log, trends — ingestion log non-negotiable for trust theatre.

### Weeks 9–12 — First cash

- Convert design partner or second brand to paying **Pulse** (35,000 ETB ex-VAT).  
- Script Signal upsell on compliance alerts + OOS.  
- Review VAT proximity and withholding process ([05](05-money.md) §7).  
- Decision gate: if shop refusal >20% in any district, pause expansion and fix access before selling harder.

## Three scripted sales conversations

### 1) Samanu design-partner (month 1)

**Open:** "You already own roughly ten of the forty staples we track every week across 120 Addis shops — oil, pasta, soap, flour, water."  
**Proof:** show one SKU district heatmap + pending outlier with readable reason.  
**Ask:** three-month pilot at 50% (or free for logo/testimonial); weekly PDF + dashboard seats for trade team.  
**Land:** "Unauthorised markups are already a Sagaci-class finding (~25% MRP compliance). We make that weekly and shop-level."

### 2) Second brand Pulse (month 4)

**Open:** buyer's alternative — field-agency style coverage of 120 shops monthly is **186k–468k ETB** as an international bound; our Pulse is **35k ex-VAT** on a shared panel.  
**Primary value:** shared fixed cost — the panel exists whether they buy or not; they rent a slice.  
**Ask:** Pulse on their category; review in week 6 for Signal.

### 3) Institutional pipeline (month 1 conversation, month 9 cash)

**Open:** WFP-class F2F questionnaire economics **$20–40** with long cycles; we resolve 4,800 cells weekly at **5.9 ETB** fully loaded per observation.  
**Ask:** not a PO — a technical evaluation slot and data-sharing MoU path.  
**Do not** discount brand seats to win a logo that pays in year two.

## Risk register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shop access / refusal** (159/2024 optics) | High | High | Letters, badges, badge programme, 10% refusal model, district reserves |
| Ambassador fraud / ghost visits | Medium | High | GPS 50m, photo hash, 10% spot audit, bonus at 95% approval |
| Design partner stalls | Medium | Medium | Parallel Phibela track; Pulse small-ticket second brand |
| Gemini cost spike / model change | Low | Low | Medium resolution enforced; budget 0.05 ETB/obs still ≪ labour |
| Withholding/VAT surprise | Medium | Medium | Ex-VAT card; register watch at 2M; cash break-even at ~2.1 seats |
| Institutional distraction | Medium | Medium | Pipeline ≠ revenue; brands fund months 1–8 |
| Crowd abuse | Medium | Low | Caps 300/day 2000/month; reputation downgrade |
| Scope creep (points, payouts, GPS polish) | High | Medium | Demo freeze list in [04] appendix; manual ops OK |

Lead risk in every review: **shop access**.
