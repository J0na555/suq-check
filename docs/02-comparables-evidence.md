# 02 — Comparables and evidence

Ten external references with usable numbers. Split into **models to copy** and **failures to design around**. FX: **158 ETB/USD** unless noted ([05-money.md](05-money.md)).

---

## A. Models to copy

### 1. Premise (crowd observation economics)

- Published cost-per-capture roughly **$0.06–0.40** → **9.5–63 ETB** at 158.  
- Lesson: AI + crowd can drive capture cost toward cents; SuqCheck's AI line is **~0.05 ETB/observation** ([05](05-money.md) §3).  
- Limit: Premise is a global observation network, not an Addis brand-compliance panel.

### 2. Trax (retail audit SaaS)

- Image recognition for shelf execution; estimated contract band **$15,000–40,000/month** ([ASSUMPTION] public range from industry write-ups — confirm before naming a customer).  
- Lesson: brands pay recurring SaaS for store truth.  
- Limit: **does not operate in Ethiopia**; price point is an order of magnitude above SuqCheck Pulse/Signal.

### 3. Bizom (retail execution)

- Public list-style pricing around **$3,000/month** → ~**474,000 ETB**.  
- Lesson: execution platforms monetize distributor/retailer workflows.  
- Limit: wrong unit of work for a 40-SKU staple compliance feed.

### 4. Fetch (brand-funded rewards)

- Brands fund receipt/reward pools as marketing spend; the platform takes infrastructure + margin.  
- Lesson: **gap-task campaigns** on SuqCheck (pool + 30% management fee, min pool 25,000 ETB) copy this mechanic so rewards are not always on our opex line ([05](05-money.md) §6).

### 5. Field Agent South Africa (mission rates)

- Retail audit missions roughly **R100–400**; live "Stock & Price Audit" about **R160–180**.  
- At ~**9.74 ETB/ZAR** → about **1,550–3,900 ETB per store mission**.  
- Lesson: density + mandated price-list photo is why SuqCheck hits **90 ETB/visit** for ~28 prices.  
- Use in pitch only as a **labelled international upper bound**, not the primary pricing argument ([05](05-money.md) changelog).

### 6. Sagaci Research (African retail)

- Syndicated / audit-style tracking across African markets.  
- Compliance hook: Sagaci-reported pattern that only about **25% of retailers complied with manufacturer recommended price**, with **~75% above** — the sales-meeting line for Samanu/Phibela.  
- Lesson: brands already buy the *category* of insight; SuqCheck sells **weekly shop-level** continuity on a fixed panel.

### 7. WFP mVAM (humanitarian price collection)

- Face-to-face price questionnaire **$20–40** → **3,160–6,320 ETB**, often with **~six-week** turnaround (WFP mVAM HIF evaluation materials).  
- SMS **$3–6**; IVR/live call **$7–9**.  
- Airtime incentives around **$0.50** used historically — precedent for Ethio telecom top-ups as a payout rail.  
- Lesson: institutional buyers understand cost-per-questionnaire maths; SuqCheck's **5.9 ETB fully loaded observation** is the contrast figure.

### 8. World Bank Retail Price / RTP-style programmes

- Multi-country efforts to modernize retail price collection for statistics and policy.  
- Lesson: procurement cycles run **6–12 months** — open institutional pipeline in **month 1** even if cash lands months 9–12 ([03-business-model.md](03-business-model.md)).  
- Limit: not a substitute for brand MRR in year one.

### 9. Nigeria NBS (national statistics price collection)

- Large enumerator networks for CPI/price stats; cost and latency are structural.  
- Lesson: national stats offices are distribution partners or data buyers later, not week-4 revenue.  
- Design note: SuqCheck stays a **panel product** first; do not promise CPI replacement.

## B. Failures to design around

### 10a. Esoko (Ghana / regional agri prices)

- Market price info dependent on **paid enumerators**, thin willingness-to-pay from farmers, weak recurring B2B anchor for the price product line.  
- Design response: **ambassador panel is sufficient for 100% shop coverage**; crowd is upside; **brands pay first**. Never claim consumer adoption is required for the map to be green.

### 10b. M-Farm (Kenya)

- Farmer price transparency with smartphone ambitions; struggled to convert information into durable revenue and ops discipline.  
- Design response: sell **compliance and distribution truth to manufacturers**, not "empowerment" as the P&L.

### 10c. mySupermarket (UK)

- Consumer aggregation / advocacy; collapsed when retail and affiliate economics did not fund the crawl.  
- Design response: consumer app is a **collection + trust surface**, not the revenue centre.

### 10d. Streetbees (global qualitative crowd)

- Rich consumer missions; expensive, project-shaped, not a weekly SKU×shop matrix.  
- Design response: keep missions as **gap-task add-ons** funded by brands (Fetch pattern), not the spine.

---

## One-line synthesis

Copy Premise/Fetch economics and Trax/Sagaci willingness-to-pay; price against WFP and Field Agent unit costs; **do not** build Esoko's enumerator-only, grant-shaped price product.
